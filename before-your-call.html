Here's something I've noticed over the years. When an owner signs with me, we're usually the third or fourth firm they've tried.

The ones before weren't lazy, but they were generic. They sent a report every month: cost per lead, clicks, reach. Some of them were charged $5k for social media marketing videos that flopped. And the classes stayed half-empty, because a lead isn't an enrolled student.

Most agencies stop at the lead. Closing becomes your problem, and I don't think it should be.

And the timing right now matters. Parents choose next year's centre between October and December. P6s may have already left after the PSLE, Sec 4s after the O Levels, K2s for Primary 1. The seats you fill in January get decided now, months before they're empty.

What we'll do in 45 minutes

Before we meet, my team goes through your website, Google reviews, social pages and ads. Then we work out three numbers together:

  • What one new student costs you today, from first ad to first payment.
  • How many empty seats you're carrying, and what they're worth in a year of fees.
  • Where parents drop off between their first message and their first payment.

Most owners have never seen those three side by side. Once you have, the rest of the conversation gets short.

Bring two things

Your numbers. Rough is fine. Exact is better. One owner who brought a spreadsheet to my last round of calls got me started faster than the ones scrambling to find theirs.

Have these open on the call

  • Enquiries you get a month
  • What you spend on ads a month
  • Out of 10 trials or tours, how many enrol
  • Students today, and how many you can fit
  • Where you want to be by next December

The person you'd run this past. Partner, spouse, co-founder, whoever. I've had too many good calls end with "let me check with my partner", and then you're explaining it all second-hand. If they're on the call, you can decide together. If whoever signs off on this can't make it, move the call. I'd rather wait a week than run it twice.

Straight answers to what owners, CFOs, CMOs and Principals ask me

"Another agency quoted us $800 a month."

For sure. There are agencies far cheaper than us. At $800 you get ads run. You don't get someone who knows why a parent picks your centre over the one down the street, or who'll fix the WhatsApp reply that's losing you half your trials. We only work with education businesses and only market strictly to parents.

"We'll do it in-house."

You can, and I'd love for you to eventually. But an in-house marketing team worth $15k/month on your costline is writing, designing, buying media and reporting. And if they leave, you start again from zero. That's excluding ad spend too. My team and I have done this for 100+ education centres over 8+ years. If you intend to form your own in-house marketing team, we can train them and hand your workflows, assets and spreadsheets over towards the end of the engagement. We'll cover the details on the call.

"Agencies are impossible to track."

Yep. That's why we're not an agency. Only an education business advisory firm will report their numbers in this manner. Say, for example, we spent $10,000: it brought 200 leads, 60 came for a trial, 20 enrolled. You get that every Friday from week 5. A generic marketing agency will not care, because they're on a retainer.

"Will I work with you, or a sales team?"

You'll work with my team leads, Sean and Jordan, and their writers, designers and media buyers. ZERO outsourcing. Everyone's trained by me, and you can always reach me.

The investment, up front

We don't run a single ad. Then we build, test and scale. If you want fast, frantic action from us, we're NOT a good fit. A skyscraper can't be built on a shaky foundation.
